Operate the Ohio fleet

Select an aircraft to frame its complete mission. Follow aircraft tracks it while you orbit and zoom. Stop following leaves the camera free. Mission outline restores the full route. Global playback speed advances simulation time; pause freezes the fleet.

Auto-deployed flights begin partway through their routes. Prior modeled tower passages and fully passed spans receive seeded camera and canopy evidence when opened. This is hypothetical history, not reconstructed sensor data. Replay from launch clears current coverage; Back to live restores the saved flight. The history estimate uses route distance, so varying speeds and repeated passes are approximate.

Return home calculates a curved connection to a backtrack route. Hover or keyboard-focus the button for a gold preview; clicking issues the simulated command. If no connection is found, the current route continues with a warning. The remainder and airport approach are not clearance validated.

Launch, landing, taxi, securing anchors, upload and solar charging are concept stages. Battery, solar yield, Starlink throughput, airports and communications are demonstration assumptions. Double-pass assignments attempt opposite-direction camera coverage if the distance budget allows it.

Profile starts collapsed. Use Show profile, then select a level or click a map buffer/clip. The scrubber previews the displayed interval. Findings in SOCC/Span view use asset position rather than processing time; clicking opens the span and finding. Mission view preserves event-time seeking.

CB, XMFR, ANGLE and EXT badges identify core sector edges. Click to focus the boundary; hover for its role. Adjacent sectors share boundary structures and one overlap span on each side. The sector split is synthetic: breaker, transformer and extension annotations are seeded, and angle structures are inferred from modeled alignment.

Empty span floor: incomplete scan. Yellow: pending imagery or intermediate finding. Red: high-priority finding/exclusion. Green: completed imagery with no current findings. Full span fill waits for along-track coverage cells, endpoint neighborhoods and processing delay. It does not certify measured LiDAR density or engineering clearance.

Camera, LiDAR and findings

Sag lines and Sway lines are simple toggles showing fixed illustrative envelopes: 3 m additional sag and ±3 m lateral sway, with fixed endpoints. Clearance zones stay around the nominal conductors. These overlays are not engineering calculations.

The right-hand Follow-up tasks list creates browser-local drafts from detected findings. Equipment drafts request multirotor overview and opposing oblique component imagery; vegetation drafts request a ground survey and qualified treatment review. Click to open the associated finding, change task status, or export JSON. Tasks are not dispatched and do not contain validated flight paths or approved vegetation prescriptions. Switching aircraft recreates this task list; export drafts to retain them.

Span vision offers Points + ghost geometry, Scene + points, and Elevation gradient. Elevation colors the 3D LiDAR and local ground from blue through teal and yellow to orange using a shared 0–60+ m height scale. The camera stays in 3D. Synthetic ground is flat and therefore has a uniform zero-height color; no terrain relief is invented.

Span inspection loads a local surface-sampled cloud on demand. Detailed sampling is always used and attempts 100,000 surface samples; the displayed count excludes samples outside the span buffer. Toggle LiDAR points and Geometry independently. Cyan conductor curves, amber support outlines and green canopy envelopes come from the synthetic scene, not point-cloud reconstruction. Points are illustrative, nonuniform and not calibrated density or occlusion simulation. An additional classified cloud adds broadleaf crowns, conifers, dead/snags, rock outcrops and box-shaped built obstacles, with per-class toggles. Classes are seeded examples, not inferred species or automated geological classification. Detailed mode attempts 300,000 total points; Leaving the span cancels generation and releases its detail layer.

Camera & sensors controls targeting, FOV and overlays. Known-coordinate mode prepositions toward the next asset; wide-view mode demonstrates reacquisition. Slew, zoom, settling and forward visibility gate captures. Gimbal timing is provisional; a skipped capture is not automatically successful.

LiDAR targets a modeled 30 points/m² budget, but the short visible trail is heavily thinned. It is not a full-density export. Synthetic tree crowns become extruded polygons after observation. Species candidates are morphology rules, not a trained classifier. Vegetation thresholds are illustrative and are not flashover limits.

Matching camera captures reveal seeded broken insulators, corrosion, hardware/crossarm and splice issues. Layers & findings opens details and review/dismiss controls. Roughly half the seeded corridor can become green after complete review; there is no forced success quota.

Voltage review and thermal DLR

The Profile side pane shows a voltage ladder and limiting phase-spacing, ground/sag, insulation and hardware envelopes. These are seeded hypothetical design capacities, not measured acceptance tests. Verified qualification remains unknown. Click a limiting tower pair to inspect that span.

Thermal DLR is separate: a simplified heat balance with synthetic wind, temperature and solar heating. SOCC ampacity is the minimum modeled span ampacity. Nominal voltage converts amps to MVA; it does not determine conductor ampacity. No actual loading or operational headroom is provided.

Real ratings require conductor specifications, approved insulation and clearance criteria, surveyed geometry, weather telemetry, terminal limits and engineering validation.

Traffic, shielding and avoidance

Traffic defaults to synthetic airplanes/helicopters with short trails, telemetry and SIM labels. Live ADS-B requires the Rust ingestion service and configured provider access. Remote ID needs a receiver/provider; the page does not directly receive radio broadcasts. NOTAM/TFR demonstrations are not an authoritative flight briefing.

Test avoidance offers crossing, oncoming and blocked scenarios. It checks candidate connections against modeled wires and an intruder trajectory. It is not a certified DAA system or live automatic traffic response. Shielding displays lateral and vertical distance to loaded assets; unknown heights remain unverified.

The 700 ft turn-radius approximation and 700 ft lateral envelope are different constraints. Route curvature does not establish obstacle clearance. Terrain, buildings, vegetation, weather and airport approach are not comprehensively validated.

Layers, exports and provenance

Basemaps include grid, topo and dark aerial imagery. Nearby OSM loading adds available buildings, power assets, trees, waterways, roads and paths. Missing heights are assumptions; OSM coverage varies. Inspection recency is seeded corridor history.

Data objects exposes nested mission and Utility Network-inspired objects. UN geometry contains synthetic supports, phase attachments, sagging conductors and splices along OSM alignments. It is a GeoJSON convention, not a validated Esri Utility Network geodatabase. SOCC exports retain shared endpoint topology and review overlap.

OSM is ODbL; aerial imagery uses USGS/USDA sources. No real asset defect, species identification or two-sided inspection completeness is implied by the examples.

Flightline mission sandbox

Open Flightline.html for editable navigation/photo waypoints, home, exclusion polygons and fixed-wing simulation. Draw an exclusion and finish it with its finish control; select it to edit vertices. Export/import mission JSON to preserve a scenario.

The bounded planar planner checks sampled turns and exclusion clearance. No-route means this search found no candidate, not proof that none exists. Its staged return protocols are illustrative and do not command a real aircraft.

Run, files and troubleshooting

Open OhioFleet.html directly on Mac. Reload after an update. Keep documentation.html beside it for this guide. Network basemaps, OSM and live ingestion require connectivity; the offline grid remains available.

Developers: npm ci, npm run dev, then open /ohio.html. Validate with npm test. Build with npm run build:fleet and package with python3 scripts/fleet_standalone.py.
